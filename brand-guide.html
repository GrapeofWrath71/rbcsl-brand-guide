Palette Usage Rules

Three palettes, one brand. These rules keep the sub-brands energetic without letting the site drift apart.

Do
  • • Keep nav, footer, and body text on the core palette on every page.
  • • Scope the children's accents to children's pages, teen accents to teen pages.
  • • Use bright accents for badges, tiles, and highlights — small doses.
  • • Alternate Warm Cream and Cool Mist to give pages rhythm.
  • • Put Onyx or Forest Shadow text on yellow, amber, green, and teal.
Don't
  • • Mix the children's and teen accents in the same section.
  • • Bring teen darks or electric teal onto the homepage or core pages.
  • • Use a sub-brand color for a primary CTA outside its own program.
  • • Set white text on Sunbeam Yellow, Meadow Green, or Signal Amber — it fails contrast.
  • • Introduce any hue not listed on this page.

Home, About, Community, Events, Give, Contact. Calm, grounded, timeless.
